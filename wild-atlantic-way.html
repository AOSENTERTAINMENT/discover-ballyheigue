The Wild Atlantic Way

Your base for the quieter northern stretch of Ireland's great coastal route.

Base yourself here, explore the coast.

Kerry has the longest stretch of the Wild Atlantic Way. Ballyheigue sits in the middle of its quieter north.

Spend the morning on the beach, take the Kerry Head loop after lunch, and you're never more than an hour from Dingle or the cliffs at Ballybunion.

The North Kerry Way

A signposted 48km trail from Tralee to Ballyheigue. Walk it over two days, or pick up any section. The final stretch loops Kerry Head past Maulin Mountain and the Cahercarbery forts.

Getting here

On the R551, 25 minutes north-west of Tralee through Ardfert. Kerry Airport is about 40 minutes away, Shannon around 1 hour 20.

Pack layers

Atlantic weather changes fast. A waterproof is never wasted, even in July.

Swim between the flags

Lifeguards cover the beach in the bathing season. Check the notice board on arrival.

Book summer early

Festival weeks fill the village, and the best beds go first.
