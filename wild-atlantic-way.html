The Wild Atlantic Way

Ballyheigue is your base for Kerry's longest stretch of Ireland's great coastal route.

Base yourself here, explore the coast.

Kerry has the longest stretch of the Wild Atlantic Way, and Ballyheigue sits right in the middle of its quieter northern half.

Spend the morning on the beach, take the Kerry Head loop after lunch, and you're never more than an hour from Dingle, Killarney or the cliffs at Ballybunion. Come back to the village for dinner and sunset.

The North Kerry Way

A signposted 48km walking trail from Tralee to Ballyheigue, joining the Dingle Way at its start. Walk it over two days, or pick up any section. The final stretch loops around Kerry Head past Maulin Mountain (218m) and the Cahercarbery forts.

Getting here

Ballyheigue is on the R551, 25 minutes north-west of Tralee through Ardfert. Kerry Airport is about 40 minutes away, Shannon around 1 hour 20 minutes. Trains and buses run to Tralee.

Along the coast

Approximate distances from Ballyheigue.

  1. Ballybunion

    About 35 km north

    Cliff walks, sea stacks, a surf school on the south beach and the famous links.

  2. Ballyheigue

    You are here

    Blue Flag beach, Kerry Head and the end of the North Kerry Way.

  3. Banna Strand

    About 8 km south

    Ten kilometres of dunes and sand linking right back to Ballyheigue. Surf lessons in summer.

  4. Ardfert Cathedral

    About 10 km

    Medieval cathedral ruins and one of Kerry's oldest Christian sites.

  5. Fenit

    About 20 km by road

    Harbour village with a lighthouse, sailing, sea angling charters and a beach café.

  6. Tralee

    25 minutes

    Kerry's county town: museum, the Rose of Tralee, shops, restaurants and the train.

  7. The Dingle Peninsula

    About an hour

    Inch, Castlegregory, the Conor Pass and Dingle town. A perfect day trip west.

Good to know

Pack layers

Atlantic weather changes fast. A waterproof is never wasted.

Swim between the flags

Lifeguards cover the beach in the bathing season. Check the notice board.

Book summer early

July brings the Ballyheigue Summer Festival and Races, and beds go fast.

Stay for sunset.
